What is Movementz Factory

A Dance Studio Built for Every Age

Movementz Factory is the dance division of M² Studios — a dedicated space in Coimbatore where kids, teens and adults train across multiple styles.

From a child's first creative movement class to adult fitness and stage-ready choreography, every batch is structured, progressive and taught by certified instructors. One simple monthly plan covers 12 classes, and the first class is on us.
